# The five constraints, tested for a mechanism

Generated by `constraints.py`. The question is not whether each constraint is satisfied — `verify.py` answers that — but whether it has a mechanism, what the arithmetic says its threshold should be, and how much margin the design has.

```
Testing the five constraints for a mechanism
requirement for isolation: -54 dB per pair (docs/00-current-state.md)
numbered as hardware-spec-v0.md section 5 numbers them, which is also
what verify.py prints and what every 'constraint N' in design.py means

1. the module draws nothing from VREG, V+ or V-
   mechanism   65 mV of negative rail per mA (55 ohm pump + 10 ohm filter)
   headroom    7.84 dB now, against the 6 dB check_headroom() demands
   threshold   21.8 mA could be drawn before that check fails
   but         212 uA IS drawn indirectly -- the mixer's summer sources our
               signal current from its own rails, so 'nothing' is unachievable (13.8 uV of sag)
   verdict     KEEP, reword

2. exactly one AGND bond, and six separate returns
   2a is binary and has no margin to compute: verify.py holds it
   2b is struck for having no mechanism -- see design.FRONT_R and the table

3. SIN{n} carries zero DC by construction
   mechanism   DC through the master pot wiper, via C703 and R706's 1M
   ours        0.50 mV residual -> 3.0 nA at the wiper
   worst case  18.0 nA at the top of servo_vos's declared range
   theirs      0.2 to 1.0 nA from U1B's own offset
   so          3x the mixer's own worst case, and the absolute threshold is unsourced
   verdict     KEEP, restate as a current

4. PIN{n} sees ~5-10 kohm, or the 31.8 Hz DC-block corner moves
   load      corner     at 5 Hz    at 55 Hz
     5000   31.83 Hz   -16.18 dB    -1.25 dB
    10000   15.92 Hz   -10.47 dB    -0.35 dB
   the sentence names 31.8 Hz, which is the 5k end; we are at 15.9 Hz
   so it is unsatisfiable anywhere in 5-10k except at exactly 5k
   choosing the top gives back +5.72 dB of subsonic rejection
   and recovers +0.91 dB at 55 Hz
   verdict     KEEP, fix the number

5. individually-shielded twisted triads, shields at one end
   PIN(n) impedance at 20 kHz  8 ohm  -- NOT 10k: see pin_impedance()
   channel-to-channel           -112.9 dB   (59 dB inside the requirement)
   the same, computed at 10k     -51.0 dB   <- the near miss: this FAILS -54 dB

   mixer charge pump             -94.3 dB   at 45 kHz
   module DC-DC                  -91.8 dB   at 300 kHz
   verdict     KEEP as practice, demote from the list

5b. the same coupling, between two traces on the board
    constraint 5 asks this of the loom; nothing asked it of the copper
   IVOUT3/PIN5       226.0 mm at 0.40 mm pitch, into 8 ohm
   BUF5/CVX1          20.6 mm at 0.93 mm pitch, into 142 ohm
   capacitive, at h = 0.1855 mm  -112.5 dB   (58 dB inside the requirement)
   the same across the sweep    -109.0 dB   the height is worth 3.5 dB, and rules.FAB_STACKUP is what settled it

5c. audio copper that is not over its own ground plane

   PIN6               32.2 mm past y = 156.44
   IVOUT4             22.2 mm past y = 156.44
   SIN4               20.3 mm past y = 156.44
   PIN2               11.6 mm past y = 156.44
   SIN2                7.6 mm past y = 156.44
   PIN4                7.1 mm past y = 156.44
   IVOUT2              6.3 mm past y = 156.44
   SIN6                6.1 mm past y = 156.44
   IVOUT6              5.0 mm past y = 156.44

   then SIN2 came back as a detour rather than a necessity -- 33.5 mm to reach a relay pad 5 mm past the pour edge, re-laid by krt.py --nets at 7.6

5d. where an audio signal changes reference plane
    In1 and In2 carry the same nets in the same places, so every via changes plane as well as layer and the return current has to transfer
   audio vias         222   against 294 ground vias
   worst gap         4.90 mm  median 1.50, 194 of 222 within 2 mm
   loop area        370.7 mm2 against 370.7 declared -- a ratchet, at 1.03 mm of plane separation
   was            1990.6 mm2 before returns.py, at a median of 7.39 mm and a worst of 22.76
   not an impedance: 4.90 mm of In1/In2 pair is tens of nH, and tens of nV at 20 kHz
   as a pickup loop it reaches the mixer's 144 uV floor at 106.6 nT of 580 kHz field
   the field is the term nothing here derives -- board_coupling() solves trace against trace, and an aggressor inside a potted brick has no drawing

5e. the domain crossings, as aggressors and as return paths
    every measurement above this line filters to the audio nets; check_crossings() reads the netlist and knows which signals cross and nothing about where
   MCLK/SIN6          30.2 mm at 0.428 mm pitch on F.Cu

   total  244.2 mm against 244.2 declared -- a ratchet, and MCLK/SIN6 is the one aggressor that runs continuously

   verdict     good practice, not load-bearing
```

| # | constraint | mechanism | margin | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | draws nothing from VREG/V+/V- | real: 65 mV of rail per mA | ~100x | **keep, reword** -- "nothing" is unachievable, since the mixer's summer sources this module's signal current from its own rails. What is meant, and what is checked, is no *supply* current. |
| 2a | exactly one AGND bond | real: a loop enclosing the mixer's pour and the loom | n/a, binary | **keep** -- the mixer's own `_GROUND_RULE` across the connector. |
| 2b | six separate returns, not commoned | **none** | 49 dB | **struck.** See `design.FRONT_R`. |
| 3 | SIN{n} zero DC by construction | real: DC through the master wiper | ~3x the mixer's own | **keep, restate as a current.** "Zero" overstates by three orders of magnitude and "by construction" is unachievable -- a servo is feedback, and the capacitor that would be construction puts a second pole beside the mixer's own. |
| 4 | PIN{n} sees 5-10 kohm | real, and computed by `coupling_burden()` upstream | n/a | **keep, fix the number.** 31.8 Hz is the corner at 5 kohm, so the sentence is unsatisfiable anywhere else in its own window. |
| 5 | individually-shielded triads, one end | real in kind | 59 dB | **keep as practice, demote from the list.** Both loom nodes are low impedance, so the coupling the shields prevent is 59 dB inside the requirement. |

**One of five had no mechanism. Two more have the mechanism and the wrong
number. One is unsatisfiable as written. One is sound.**

That is not an argument against having the list. It is an argument for the list
being derived rather than drafted -- and for the distinction the exercise turns
on: a constraint with thin margin is load-bearing, a constraint with 59 dB of
margin is good practice, and the two do not want the same treatment. Both were
in the same list, checked with the same rigour, and only one of them was worth
it.
